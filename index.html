Small print, useful

Things worth knowing

What to wear

Kerala formal

Saree, set-mundu, kurta or suit. The church floor is stone and the courtyard may be wet — flat shoes will thank you.

If it rains

It might

Everything moves indoors except the photographs, which move under the portico. Umbrellas are provided at both venues.

Parking

Behind the parish hall

Free, with attendants from 7:30 am. The reception hall has basement parking off M.C. Road.

Staying over

Rooms held nearby

A block of rooms is held at two hotels within a kilometre. Mention the wedding when booking, or ask us and we will arrange it.

Children

Very welcome

There is a quiet room beside the sacristy for feeding and naps, and a separate table at lunch.

Gifts

Your presence, honestly

You are travelling to be with us and that is plenty. If you would still like to give something, a note at the reception desk is perfect.
